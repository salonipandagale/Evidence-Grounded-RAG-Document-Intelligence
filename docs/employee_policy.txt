
Employee Leave Policy

Full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of annual leave per calendar year.

Employees must submit a leave request at least 3 working days before the intended leave date.

Sick leave is available to full-time employees for up to 10 days per year.

Contract employees are not eligible for paid sick leave under this policy.

Employees who are absent for more than 3 consecutive working days may be required to provide supporting documentation.

Unused annual leave can be carried forward to the next calendar year, subject to a maximum of 5 days.

All leave requests must be approved by the employee's reporting manager.
